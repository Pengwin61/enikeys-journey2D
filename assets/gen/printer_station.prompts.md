# Printer station generation

Built-in `image_gen` tool, edit mode, `transparent_background: true`. Outputs inspected and copied into the workspace; original printers preserved. Each reference was inspected before generation. The two styles were generated separately.

## Painted

Reference: `assets/gen/painted/props/printer.png`.
Output: `assets/gen/painted/props/printer_station.png`.

Prompt:

> Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: a single isometric game prop sprite, printer standing securely on its own low office storage cabinet, transparent PNG. Input image is the edit target and exact printer/style reference. Preserve this multifunction office laser printer's identity, gray and ivory panels, scanner lid, front tray, blue control screen, small green LED, viewing direction, and detailed hand-painted outlined game art. ADD directly beneath it a squat sturdy charcoal-gray metal printer pedestal cabinet, two paper-storage drawers with small horizontal handles, slightly wider and deeper than the printer, four small dark feet touching the ground. Printer must sit flush on the cabinet top with believable contact, zero floating gap. Cabinet body about as tall as printer body, enough visible flat top around it. One coherent combined object, not separate objects or a sheet. Orthographic 2:1 isometric, visible front faces lower-left, matching reference exactly; no change of perspective. Dark corporate office visual style, restrained wear, cool blue-gray metal and small warm edge highlights. Full object entirely visible centered with minimal transparent margins, ground contact at image bottom. Actual alpha transparency; no floor, backdrop, huge cast shadow, words, logos, UI, watermark or other furniture. Production-ready game sprite.

## Pixel

Prompt:

> Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: one transparent PNG isometric PIXEL ART game sprite, complete printer on its matching storage pedestal. Input image is the edit target and exact pixel printer/style reference. Keep the reference printer identity, chunky pixel grid, dark crisp outlines, flat gray and light-blue shaded panels, scanner lid, cyan screen on the lower-left-facing front, paper tray and white sheets. ADD a sturdy low blue-gray metal printer cabinet directly beneath this printer, slightly wider and deeper than it, two large paper drawers with small dark horizontal handles, four small dark feet resting on ground. Drawer cabinet body roughly as tall as the printer itself. The printer base must sit FLUSH ON the cabinet top with NO AIR GAP. One assembled object with consistent geometry and pixel grid, not two disconnected objects or a sheet. Orthographic isometric 2:1 camera matches reference exactly. Clean low-resolution chunky pixel art, manually stepped pixel edges and small palette, no smooth painting, no gradient glow or photographic texture. Sprite entirely visible and centered with minimal transparent margins and ground at bottom. True alpha transparent background. No floor, environment, extended shadow, text, watermark, branding or additional furniture.
