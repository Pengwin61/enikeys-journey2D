# Coins and quest markers

Created using built-in `image_gen`, transparent-background PNGs, one call per asset. All outputs inspected and copied into the project. No source images were replaced.

## Painted coin

Saved path: `assets/gen/painted/ui/coin_token.png`.

Prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one gold corporate currency token for a dark isometric office action game. Single thick round brass coin, mostly front-facing with a slight 3/4 isometric angle so its right rim is visible. Center stamped emblem: three upward monolithic tower bars, tallest middle bar with pointed peak, shorter left and right bars. Raised dark gold rim and thick readable engraving, warm amber edge highlights, dark bronze recesses. Detailed outlined hand-painted game art, matches dark blue-gray isometric office furniture with warm amber accents. Strong simple silhouette readable at 24px UI / 8px in-world. One coin only, no stack, no sheet, no extra symbols, lettering, currency signs, numbers, watermark, background, environment or cast shadow. Fully transparent alpha outside coin, centered with minimal margins. No magical glow cloud.

## Painted quest marker

Saved path: `assets/gen/painted/ui/quest_marker.png`.

Prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: ONE quest marker HUD/game-world sprite for a dark corporate office game. A small vertically elongated dark metal badge with chamfered top corners, silver bevels, a large clearly recognizable WHITE EXCLAMATION MARK '!' filling its center, and a short downward pointed locator tip at the bottom.
